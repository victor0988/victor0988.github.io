02Measure, then improve

Two different jobs.

AI Visibility watches. Posicionamiento en IA works on the presence.

AI Visibility tracks how you appear in search and in AI answers. It does not, by itself, change that presence. Posicionamiento en IA is the service for the work of being easier to cite: the profile, the structured data, the questions you answer, reviews and third-party mentions, at the scope of the package you hire.

Nothing on this page promises a ranking, a number of citations or a revenue result. Those figures are not published because they would be invented.
