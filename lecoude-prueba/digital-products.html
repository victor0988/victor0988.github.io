01What we build

Software you subscribe to — not software you have to build.

We create and commercialize scalable digital products that help organizations access technology through flexible subscription models.

Each product focuses on one job and does it well: understanding your market, being found online, talking to customers, or running a clinic. You get mature software, ongoing updates and a team that supports it.

02Our products

Four products. Four specific problems.

Each one is ready to configure for your organization and can integrate with the systems you already use.

AI Geo

Geographic intelligence and market opportunity analytics.

AI Geo maps where your market is — and where it isn't yet. It brings territory, point-of-sale and demand signals into geographic dashboards so commercial teams can prioritize zones, plan coverage and spot untapped opportunities.

  • Territory analysis
  • Market intelligence
  • Geographic dashboards
  • Commercial opportunities
  • Location-based analytics

Built for: Commercial, sales and expansion teams in retail, distribution and services.

AI Visibility

Search and AI discovery visibility analytics.

Customers now discover brands through search engines and AI assistants. AI Visibility tracks how your brand appears across both — which queries surface you, how AI answers describe you, and where competitors are gaining ground. Measuring is the whole job of this product. AI Engage

Conversational customer engagement and automation.

AI Engage is the product name for the agent on website chat and WhatsApp. It answers questions, qualifies leads and handles routine requests, with a hand-off to your team when a person is needed. There is no second product called WhatsApp and Web AI Agent.

  • AI chat
  • WhatsApp
  • Customer support
  • Sales automation
  • Conversation analytics

Built for: Customer service and sales teams handling high message volumes.

AI Health

Digital clinical management and electronic records.

AI Health gives clinics a single place for patient records, scheduling and day-to-day administration, with role-based access so each member of the team sees exactly what they need.

  • Patient records
  • Scheduling
  • Clinical workflows
  • Administrative tools
  • Access controls

Built for: Private clinics and healthcare practices digitizing their operations.
