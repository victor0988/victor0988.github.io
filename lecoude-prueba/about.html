Lecoude designs and builds digital products, custom software, AI solutions and enterprise platforms — for organizations that need technology to work in the real world, not just in a demo.

Work with us →How we work

01Who we are

Engineers and designers who've worked inside the operations we build for.

Our work grew out of demanding operational environments — aviation ground operations, healthcare practices and commercial distribution.

There, software has to be fast, reliable and easy for busy people to use. Today we combine four practices — digital products, custom software, AI and automation, and enterprise solutions — so clients work with one accountable team instead of coordinating several vendors.

We're based in Costa Rica and work with organizations across Latin America in English and Spanish.
